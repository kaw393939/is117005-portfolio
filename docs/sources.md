# Source register

**Research access date: November 5, 2026.** Primary sources support the figures and role descriptions. Links are attached to the claims throughout the site. This register explains their provenance and limits.

All four Occupational Outlook Handbook pages below were last modified **August 27, 2026** and use **2025–2035** employment projections. They describe broad national occupations, not individual employers or every AI specialization.

## International research

- **World Economic Forum**, [*The Future of Jobs Report 2025*](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/), January 2025. [Chapter 2](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook) supports the directional AI/ML growth signal. Its 2025–2030 employer expectations differ from BLS projections in geography, time period, and method.
- **AI Workforce Consortium**, [*ICT in Motion: The Next Wave of AI Integration*](https://www.cisco.com/content/dam/cisco-cdc/site/m/ai-workforce-consortium/documents/2025-ai-workforce-consortium-full-report.pdf), September 2025. Methodology p. 19; selected-role demand table p. 24; AI product manager illustration p. 54. It analyzes 50 selected roles across G7 countries using Cornerstone and Indeed data. [Publisher announcement, September 16, 2025](https://investor.cisco.com/news/news-details/2025/AI-Workforce-Consortium-Finds-78-of-ICT-Roles-Now-Include-AI-Technical-Skills-While-Human-Skills-Gain-Priority-for-Responsible-Tech-Adoption/default.aspx).

## Responsibilities and technical context

- **Google Cloud**, [Professional Machine Learning Engineer](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/machine-learning-engineer), live certification outline, accessed November 5, 2026. Used to describe responsibilities; it is not hiring or growth evidence.
- **Amazon Web Services**, [What is MLOps?](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/mlops/), undated live explainer, accessed November 5, 2026. Used for operational tasks, not market size.
- **NIST**, [AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), framework 1.0 released January 26, 2023. Used for governance responsibilities; voluntary guidance does not establish hiring demand.
- **NIST**, [*Adversarial Machine Learning: A Taxonomy and Terminology of Attacks and Mitigations*](https://www.nist.gov/publications/adversarial-machine-learning-taxonomy-and-terminology-attacks-and-mitigations-0), March 24, 2025, NIST AI 100-2e2025. Used for AI-security concepts, not employment forecasts.

## Editorial synthesis

Suggested skills, routes, and portfolio projects are educational recommendations built from this research. They are not guaranteed hiring requirements. No live job-posting sample or local-market analysis was conducted. See the [methodology](/methodology) for the project's research rules.
