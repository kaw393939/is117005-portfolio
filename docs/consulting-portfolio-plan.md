# Consulting portfolio plan

**Status:** planning, not implementation. **Organization reviewed:** November 17, 2026.

The goal is a future portfolio that helps a prospective client understand Keith’s work, inspect evidence and begin a useful conversation. This site holds the research and decisions, not the marketing site itself.

## Review in this order

| Step | Document | Decision or question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Brief](/portfolio-brief) | Who is the first audience, and which proposed offer fits? |
| 2 | [Personas](/portfolio-personas) | What evidence would a potential employer need? |
| 3 | [Evidence](/portfolio-evidence) | Which claims can we substantiate? |
| 4 | [Backlog](/portfolio-backlog) | Which stories belong in the first release? |
| 5 | [Delivery and quality](/portfolio-delivery) | What must be decided and checked before publishing? |

## Decide before coding

- **Keith:** choose the initial audience, offer scope and private inquiry route; approve publication permissions and final claims.
- **Implementer:** inspect the current portfolio/repositories, identify dependencies, propose a small release and record verification.
- **Together:** narrow the draft backlog. Six P0 stories are not an automatic commitment to implement all six at once.

**Still unknown:** validated buyer demand, approved service packages, contact destination and any unverified outcomes. Fictional personas are hypotheses, not customer research.
