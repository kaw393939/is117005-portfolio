# The work behind the title

These profiles describe career directions, not current vacancies. Skills and routes are a practical synthesis; suggested projects are ideas to build, not claims of completed work. Employers use titles differently. The [evidence page](/research) explains which growth figures apply to broad occupations.

## AI / Machine Learning Engineer {#ml-engineer}

**Emerging title · Build**

**The work:** Develop model-based systems, evaluate results, and connect models to applications. [Google's role outline](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/machine-learning-engineer) emphasizes building, evaluating, and operating ML solutions.

**Skills:** Python, data preparation, machine learning, APIs, testing, and evaluation.

**Route:** Build programming and statistics foundations, then pursue software or data internships and ML projects. An advanced certification is not a beginner prerequisite; Google's professional exam recommends substantial industry experience.

**Portfolio idea:** Compare a simple baseline with a small classifier. Publish the dataset source, evaluation split, error examples, and an API demo.

**Evidence:** WEF identifies AI/ML specialists as a growth area. This site assigns no separate U.S. forecast to the engineer title.

## Data Scientist {#data-scientist}

**Established occupation · Data · 35% projected U.S. growth, 2025–35**

**The work:** Turn messy data into insights, test predictive approaches, and communicate uncertainty. The title spans many industries and is not exclusively an AI job.

**Skills:** Statistics, Python, SQL, visualization, experimental thinking, and clear explanations.

**Route:** BLS lists a bachelor's degree as typical entry education; some employers prefer graduate study. Analyst projects or internships can help demonstrate the foundations.

**Portfolio idea:** Investigate a public dataset, test a prediction against a baseline, and explain where the model fails.

**Evidence:** [BLS occupational profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm).

## Computer & Information Research Scientist {#research-scientist}

**Established occupation · Build · 22% projected U.S. growth, 2025–35**

**The work:** Explore computing questions and design experiments that test new methods. AI research is one specialization within this wider occupation.

**Skills:** Algorithms, mathematics, programming, reproducible experiments, and research writing.

**Route:** BLS lists a master's degree as typical entry education. Develop research experience with a faculty project or a careful reproduction of a published experiment.

**Portfolio idea:** Reproduce a small paper result, vary one assumption, and document computational cost and limitations.

**Evidence:** [BLS occupational profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm). The 22% figure covers the full occupation.

## MLOps / AI Platform Engineer {#mlops-engineer}

**Emerging title · Build**

**The work:** Automate model delivery, track versions, monitor behavior, and support reliable operations. [AWS's MLOps explanation](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/mlops/) describes these lifecycle practices.

**Skills:** Linux, containers, CI/CD, version control, deployment, monitoring, and ML basics.

**Route:** Start with software, cloud, or systems experience, then add reproducible model workflows. Production responsibilities often require prior engineering experience.

**Portfolio idea:** Package a small model, automate a deployment, log model versions, and demonstrate a rollback.

**Evidence:** Technical documentation establishes the work; it does not establish a separate occupational growth rate.

## AI Security Engineer {#ai-security-engineer}

**Emerging title · Trust**

**The work:** Examine threats to AI systems and test defenses. [NIST's adversarial ML taxonomy](https://www.nist.gov/publications/adversarial-machine-learning-taxonomy-and-terminology-attacks-and-mitigations-0) covers threats including poisoning, evasion, and privacy attacks.

**Skills:** Application security, Python, threat modeling, access controls, and adversarial evaluation.

**Route:** Establish networking and software-security fundamentals, then investigate AI-specific risks in a controlled lab. A security or software internship is a useful adjacent starting point.

**Portfolio idea:** Threat-model a local document assistant and test whether untrusted text can change its behavior. Record controls and residual failures.

**Evidence:** BLS security analyst data supplies broader context only; there is no separate forecast here for AI security engineers.

## AI Product Manager {#ai-product-manager}

**Emerging title · Strategy**

**The work:** Define a user problem, set success measures, and coordinate design and engineering around AI limitations. The consortium report illustrates this transition from conventional product management on p. 54.

**Skills:** User research, prioritization, metrics, AI literacy, and communication.

**Route:** Build experience in product analysis, business analysis, user research, or software delivery. Understand model evaluation well enough to discuss tradeoffs with engineers.

**Portfolio idea:** Write a product brief for an AI study tool, interview potential users, prototype the flow, and propose an evaluation plan.

**Evidence:** [Consortium role illustration](https://www.cisco.com/content/dam/cisco-cdc/site/m/ai-workforce-consortium/documents/2025-ai-workforce-consortium-full-report.pdf). An illustration is not a growth forecast.

## AI Risk & Governance Specialist {#ai-governance-specialist}

**Emerging title · Trust**

**The work:** Coordinate risk reviews, document decisions, and establish accountability for AI use. [NIST's AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) provides a voluntary framework for this work.

**Skills:** Risk assessment, technical literacy, documentation, stakeholder communication, and evidence review.

**Route:** Combine information systems or another domain background with risk, audit, privacy, or compliance experience. Learn how to explain system limitations to different audiences.

**Portfolio idea:** Create an AI system inventory, risk register, review record, and monitoring plan for a fictional campus assistant.

**Evidence:** The consortium reports rising demand for this title. Its G7 dataset is not a separate U.S. projection.

## Software Developer {#software-developer}

**Established occupation · Build · 10% projected U.S. growth, 2025–35**

**The work:** Build, test, and maintain applications. AI integration is one possible focus alongside many other forms of software.

**Skills:** JavaScript or TypeScript, APIs, databases, testing, accessibility, and debugging.

**Route:** BLS lists a bachelor's degree as typical entry education. A documented application and a software internship can demonstrate practical capability.

**Portfolio idea:** Build an accessible research website, then add a clearly labeled search or recommendation feature with measurable acceptance criteria.

**Evidence:** [BLS occupational profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm). The 10% forecast covers all software developers, including work unrelated to AI.
