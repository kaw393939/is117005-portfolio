# What the earlier AI Career Atlas teaches us

Reviewed November 5, 2026. Repository content was inspected read-only at commit [`b5e0ada`](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/commit/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e). No repository scripts were executed. The [README](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/README.md) identifies the published site as [AI Career Atlas](https://kaw393939.github.io/ai-career-research/). This review concerns its recorded evidence, not a fresh verification of every employer opening.

## Seven lessons to carry into this portfolio

1. **Start with work, then inspect eligibility.** The atlas connects 15 profiles across eight fields with job examples, learning routes, an NJIT map, and a worksheet. Its comparison asks what someone ships, who uses it, which skills matter, and which constraints apply. We can reuse that decision process instead of treating attractive titles as career conclusions. [Role comparison](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/roles/index.md)

2. **AI careers extend beyond model training.** Profiles cover application development, forward deployment, evaluation, platform engineering, networking, security, product, and responsible AI. The atlas explicitly says established titles and emerging responsibilities overlap. Our research should compare deliverables and skills before declaring a title a new occupation. [Role directory](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/tree/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/roles)

3. **The sample is useful evidence, with a defined boundary.** The four evidence files contain 20 examples from 19 employers: six internships, five entry-level, three mid-level, and six senior. At the recorded November 5 review, 17 were labeled “Open when checked,” two were uncertain pipelines, and one was closed. Those labels describe an observed page and application affordance, not continued hiring or market-wide demand. Retain the exact title, source, check date, requirements, and level explanation. [Evidence files](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/tree/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research), [methodology](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/methodology.md)

4. **Regional accessibility remains the clearest research gap.** Explicit New York options are Scale's research internship, Adobe's graduate ML position, Duolingo's data internship, and an expired NYC HPD internship. Scale asks for research evidence; Adobe asks for an advanced degree or equivalent experience. None of the 20 records lists a New Jersey work location. US-remote labels do not establish eligibility in every state. Our strongest addition is current NY/NJ applied-AI, FDE, software, security, and IT pathways with realistic undergraduate barriers recorded. [Internship evidence](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/internship-evidence.json), [entry evidence](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/entry-evidence.json), [expanded evidence](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/expanded-evidence.json)

5. **“Internship” does not mean beginner or eligible.** Enrollment, graduation windows, research history, travel, citizenship, and sponsorship can decide whether a description is worth pursuing. The atlas's simulated student review found that literal searches miss graduation ranges. Structured checks help, provided missing facts remain unknown and a potential match is never presented as an admission decision. [Student review](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/revisions/student-review.md), [eligibility facts](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/eligibility-facts.json)

6. **Keep occupational growth separate from job snapshots.** The atlas records BLS 2025–2035 projections and national May 2025 median wages, with occupation codes and mapping limits. These describe broad occupations across experience levels, not starting pay, local AI salaries, or growth for a particular emerging title. Preserve those units and vintages; a small employer collection cannot measure growth. [Occupational data](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/occupational-context.json)

7. **Make maintenance and decisions inspectable.** Separate JSON evidence, typed presentation data, and a source ledger are reusable patterns. The source-age report explicitly performs no network verification. The release record links observations to decisions, issues, code, checks, and deployment, while labeling simulated feedback honestly. Our additions should carry source dates and verification limits through publication. [Source review](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/research/source-review.md), [decision provenance](https://github.com/kaw393939/ai-career-research/blob/b5e0adaa4864b2ad4853a1ac7b7e0b8b8b98a20e/docs/revisions/decision-provenance.md)
