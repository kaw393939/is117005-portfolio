# What the local sample shows

The New York and New Jersey sample suggests that AI careers extend across customer delivery, security, enterprise systems, infrastructure, and business adoption. The useful distinction is the responsibility a person owns: building a model, connecting it to a workflow, operating the surrounding systems, or reviewing its risks.

These are findings from employer postings collected **November 5, 2026**, not a census of regional employment. Check each record in the [job explorer](/jobs) for its verification status and location caveats. A readable description or application link does not guarantee that recruiting remains active.

## New responsibilities inside familiar teams

Forward deployed engineering combines technical implementation with customer problem-solving. Enterprise AI engineering adds agents and integrations to existing business systems. Security teams now investigate model-facing threats alongside conventional access and application risks. Governance work connects technical behavior to organizational accountability. These responsibilities overlap; the [title map](/title-map) explains the distinctions without assuming that titles mean the same thing everywhere.

New Jersey offers examples beyond model research. CoreWeave's **Software and AI Engineer** connects agents, applications, and enterprise workflows; its requirements include at least three years each of programming and scripting. That makes it an experienced software route into applied AI. [CoreWeave posting](https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/coreweave/jobs/4719916006)

The broader interpretation is that useful AI work depends on data access, integration, deployment, maintenance, and communication. A student can demonstrate these capabilities before attempting a large model-training project.

## Entry routes exist, with concrete expectations

MUFG's Jersey City **Global Red Team AI Engineer, Analyst** explicitly targets early-career applicants and accepts evidence from internships, academic projects, or personal work. Its AI security focus still calls for practical coding and hands-on AI experience. [MUFG posting](https://mufgub.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/MUFG-Careers/job/Jersey-City-NJ/Global-Red-Team-AI-Engineer--Analyst_10080289-WD-1)

Provectus's **Associate Forward Deployed Engineer** requests zero to two years, counting internships, co-ops, and research. Applicants need AI-integrated projects and programming foundations. Although the headline includes New Jersey, attendance is required in the New York office three days weekly. [Provectus posting](https://jobs.lever.co/provectus/62394851-693e-47fe-a86c-f8d9acf43ec2)

These examples support a practical conclusion: entry level can mean limited professional tenure while still requiring demonstrable technical work. They do not establish that beginners can enter every AI specialization equally easily.

## Internships have different gates

Regeneron's **2027 Summer Intern AI/ML & Data Engineering** spans scientific and business applications, with placements including Warren, New Jersey and several New York sites. Enrollment and placement eligibility matter; some projects suit graduate students better than others. [Regeneron posting](https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/XMLNAME-2027-Summer-Intern-AI-ML---Data-Engineering_R52560-1)

Avis Budget Group's Parsippany **AI Enablement Intern** emphasizes adoption and coordination, but requests prior internship or professional experience. Campbell's Camden **IT Internship Program** lists AI/automation among possible placements and specifies a minimum 3.0 GPA; an AI assignment is not guaranteed. Compare the actual gates before treating either title as a universal beginner opportunity. ## Domain knowledge changes the work

Finance appears in customer implementation, adversarial testing, and governance. BlackRock's New York **AI Risk Oversight, Director** expects enterprise experience and the ability to challenge senior stakeholders across AI deployment and operational risk. Seniority includes judgment and organizational responsibility, beyond knowledge of a framework. [BlackRock posting](https://careers.blackrock.com/job/new-york/ai-risk-oversight-director/45831/102830380048)

Life sciences adds scientific context. Insurance also offers adjacent foundations: IAT's Newark **System Administrator Internship** covers systems, cloud operations, and automation rather than dedicated AI-model development. It can build relevant experience without being mislabeled an AI engineering internship. [IAT posting](https://iatinsurancegroup.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/iat/job/Newark-NJ/System-Administrator-Internship_JR100415)

## What to study and demonstrate

For a student portfolio, choose one direction and produce a small, inspectable result. A delivery project might connect a documented API to a useful workflow. A security project might evaluate a local assistant's permissions and failure cases. An operations project might include deployment instructions, monitoring, and a recovery procedure. Explain the user problem, your decisions, evidence of quality, and remaining limitations.

Start with programming, Git, SQL, APIs, testing, and clear documentation; add role-specific material deliberately. This is an educational synthesis, not a claim that every employer requires the same stack. The sample cannot establish vacancy totals, growth rates, hiring odds, or regional rankings. Read the [methodology](/methodology) and [earlier national research](/existing-research) for the distinction between local postings and occupational projections.
