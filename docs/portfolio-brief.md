# Portfolio brief

[← Planning overview](/consulting-portfolio-plan)

**Proposed, not approved for implementation.** Choose audience and offer scope before coding.

## Audience and outcome

**Goal:** Help a prospective client understand the problem Keith can help with, inspect relevant work, and begin a qualified conversation.

**Positioning proposal:** *Practical AI adoption, grounded in education and software engineering.* Keith’s strongest distinction is connecting teaching, real software delivery and human judgment, not claiming to automate every business problem.

**Primary audiences**

| Visitor | Decision they need to make | Evidence they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education or training leader | Can Keith help our people use AI thoughtfully? | Teaching approach, workshop example, learning outcomes and boundaries. |
| Organization or technical lead | Can he turn our AI idea into a useful, testable project? | Architecture, documentation, implementation examples and evaluation method. |
| Potential collaborator | Are our questions and ways of working compatible? | Research agenda, project maturity and a clear contact route. |

Students remain a secondary audience with a direct route to learning resources. Do not make them navigate a sales funnel to find the research.



## Offer hypotheses

These are **offer hypotheses for Keith to review**, not existing packages, prices or delivery commitments.

1. **AI learning and workshops:** help a team understand tools, practice on a relevant task and evaluate its results. Proposed output: workshop plan, exercises and an evaluation checklist.
2. **AI workflow and prototype planning:** turn an unclear idea into a bounded use case, evidence requirements, architecture and testable pilot. Proposed output: decision brief, prototype scope and acceptance criteria.
3. **Educational experience design:** improve course or training information architecture, assignments and assessment. Proposed output: content audit, redesigned sample and implementation plan.

Each service should explain who it helps, the problem, proposed deliverables, what is outside scope and the first step. No guaranteed business results, universal timeline or pricing until approved.

## Proposed page structure

Use at most five primary navigation items: **Services · Work · Insights · About · Contact**.

| Page | Question answered | Essential content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Is this relevant to me? | Compact value proposition, three service summaries, selected evidence and one primary contact link. No oversized hero, carousel or autoplay. |
| Services | What could we do together? | Audience, problem, output, boundaries, process and relevant work for each offer. |
| Work | What has he actually done? | Case studies with role, context, artifact, observed result, limitations and source. |
| Insights | How does he think? | Curated research and useful teaching resources, dates and evidence links; not an unfiltered archive. |
| About | Why trust him? | Verified experience, approachable personal story, teaching/building approach and professional links. |
| Contact | How do we start? | Approved private contact route, what information is helpful, and a warning not to send sensitive data. |

Case-study detail pages sit below Work. Existing learning and research URLs remain reachable. A public GitHub issue is not the default destination for confidential consulting inquiries.
