## What is collected

- **26 postings from 14 employers:** 7 internships, 2 entry-level, 3 mid-level, and 14 senior examples in NY/NJ.
- **Role descriptions:** responsibilities, skills, entry routes, and project ideas.
- **Context:** occupational projections and a [review of the earlier Career Atlas](/existing-research).

**Evidence last reviewed: November 5, 2026.** These are dated examples, not verified openings today. Counts describe this selected sample, not the whole job market. The site organization was updated November 17; the research was not re-verified.

## A short research session

1. Pick one role family and compare three relevant postings.
2. Record required versus preferred skills, eligibility, source dates, and unknowns.
3. Choose one next step in the [planning worksheet](/plan).
4. Ask a partner to check the evidence and navigation. Track any correction in a GitHub issue.

## Open questions

- Which postings are still open, and what are their application deadlines?
- Which requirements recur across employers rather than appearing in only one example?
- Which options fit my current experience, location, and available time?
- Where are salary, eligibility, or skill requirements missing from the evidence?

Keep unanswered questions visible. Do not turn missing information into a confident conclusion.
