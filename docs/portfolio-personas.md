# Potential employer personas

[← Planning overview](/consulting-portfolio-plan)

Use these to review whether a page helps a hiring decision, not to invent market demand.

**Fictional planning personas, not interviewed people or confirmed buyers.** These describe decision makers who might hire Keith for a role or consulting engagement. Their goals and concerns are hypotheses to test, not findings from the job sample.

#### Maya — Director of learning and workforce development

- **Hiring trigger:** her organization wants staff to use AI, but demonstrations have not translated into dependable day-to-day practice.
- **Goal:** find an educator who can turn a real workflow into practical training with clear learning checks.
- **Decision role:** recommends the educator or consultant; budget owners and procurement approve the engagement.
- **Concerns:** hype, generic workshops, inaccessible materials and unsafe handling of organizational data.
- **Evidence she needs:** one inspectable workshop or course example, Keith’s exact contribution, exercises, assessment method and honest outcome limits. NJIT’s [2019 workshop report](https://news.njit.edu/inspiring-students-njit-africa) and [2023 teaching report](https://news.njit.edu/njit-faculty-artificial-intelligence-learning-opportunity) support exploring this fit, not claims of measured client ROI.
- **Portfolio implication:** lead with a concrete learning problem and a short teaching case; offer a conversation about a bounded workshop rather than broad transformation promises.

#### Jordan — Head of applied AI delivery

- **Hiring trigger:** a team needs to move an AI idea beyond a demo into a testable workflow that people can use.
- **Goal:** find someone who connects user needs, technical decisions, evaluation and team communication.
- **Decision role:** assesses technical and delivery fit; recruiting, security and budget owners may also participate.
- **Concerns:** polished demos without maintainable code, unclear personal contribution, unsupported performance claims and hidden operational costs.
- **Evidence they need:** a runnable artifact, architecture tradeoffs, tests/evaluation, documented limitations and a clear handoff. The [regional source ledger](/job-sources) contains forward-deployed and applied-AI role examples; it does not establish demand for Keith’s services or his qualification for any specific vacancy.
- **Portfolio implication:** show one verified software case with setup, evaluation and maturity status. Keep planned capabilities separate from delivered work, then invite discussion of a scoped pilot.

**Validation before expanding scope:** ask a representative decision maker from each audience what evidence is missing and what would justify a first conversation. Record actual feedback separately from these fictional profiles.
