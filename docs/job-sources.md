# Employer source ledger

**Reviewed November 5, 2026.** This selected sample contains **26 postings from 14 employers**: 7 internships, 2 entry-level, 3 mid-level, and 14 senior. Entries are research examples, not a complete vacancy count. Some assignments offer multiple locations; 19 list a New York worksite and 10 list a New Jersey worksite, with three counted in both. Provectus is counted as NY because its attendance requirement specifies the New York office.

For 23 records, Chrome displayed the employer description and an Apply control. Three CoreWeave records were confirmed through the employer's official ATS JSON in Chrome; the public job template did not display their descriptions, so an application form was not verified. **Application link available when checked** records that observation only; it does not confirm continued recruiting or an accepted application.

## Exclusions and corrections

- The earlier MUFG analyst requisition **10077350-WD** no longer displayed a job. The live board led to **10080289-WD**, which is the record included here.
- Turing's training-role link did not produce an available job description during discovery and is excluded. Its Field Engineer, AI Labs example has conflicting NYC/San Francisco location signals and is excluded from the local inventory; it remains a useful geography caveat in the title discussion.
- A Merck internship discovered during research had an October 9, 2026 deadline and was excluded as past its deadline.
- General IT and security pathways are labeled **Adjacent pathway** when the posting does not establish dedicated AI duties. Company branding alone does not make a role AI engineering.
- Workday links were corrected to the employer's actual location-bearing job URLs. A search snippet's shortened URL was insufficient for several records.

The discovery files in the repository preserve candidate notes. The reviewed dataset drives this ledger and the job explorer. See the [methodology](/methodology) for classification rules and sampling limits.
